Communications intercept. Transmission detected Wed 16th of Apr 2008 at 07:31 AM by Othinn. Communique follows:





LED Black Box

This code hack, first used by the General's commandos disguised as Agents, boosts your attack power. It will fall to the ground if you are killed or leave the district. You can be attacked by other operatives as long as it is in your possession.




N30 Frag

This code hack, allegedly originating from a bizarre combination of Neo's RSI code and the General's commandos disguised as Agents, increases your damage resistance. It will fall to the ground if you are killed or leave the district. You can be attacked by other operatives as long as it is in your possession.




Bomb Pet

This code hack, originally used by Morpheus, helps guard you against viral attacks, and boosts your own viral attack capabilities. It will fall to the ground if you are killed or leave the district. You can be attacked by other operatives as long as it is in your possession.




Portable Signal Booster

This portable signal booster does not function when carried. You must drop it to benefit from it. It is slightly less effective than a normal signal booster.




Quicksilver Compilation Module

This luggable greatly enhances the speed and reduces the cost of compiling items, as well as increasing all of your crafting skills a small amount.




Decompiler Overdrive Module

This luggable allows you to decompile items very quickly and increases your chances of success. It also increases your crafting skills slightly.




Code Optimizer

This luggable optimizes your ability codes, allowing you to use them for less inner strength. It also boosts your maximum inner strength. Using this luggable from your inventory converts it into Optimizer Fragments.




Overdrive Unit

This luggable ramps up your adrenaline production and heightens aggression. This has the effect of increasing your damage output significantly, but with the side effect of enraging you until the luggable is taken or released. This luggable can be used from your inventory to convert it into Overdrive Fragments.




Quicksilver

This luggable enhances your speed and reactions, enabling you to block and evade attacks more easily. Using this luggable from your inventory will convert it into Quicksilver Fragments.




Smith Virus Virion

This concentrated mass of the Smith Virus imparts both great power and madness. Picking it up will make you vulnerable to attack by other players.
