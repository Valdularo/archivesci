Sundog: I represent the broadcast facility at Radio Free Zion - if you have any messages you would like broadcast to the masses of humanity who tune into our feed, feel free to contact me directly at this number.

Sundog: This is a theory that we have been discussing here as well.

Niobe: And who has ever heard of a "platoon" of Agents? Something's not right here.

Sundog: Definitely reeks of misdirection. What are your plans in light of this possibility?

Niobe: We need you to hit the streets and figure out what it's all about. Try to infiltrate some Machine and Merovingian factions, and discover if either of these Organizations is behind the broadcast.

Sundog: I will have my staff trying to find any clues to the origin of the broadcast and will contact you if we come across any new information. I would ask that you keep us informed as well.

Niobe: Remember, unless someone attacks you first, stay calm. Until you hear otherwise the Truce is still in effect.

Sundog: We will heed your advice - let me know if you need our further assistance at any time through this communications channel.

Niobe: If I find out anything on my end, I will contact you immediately. - Niobe

Sundog: Stay safe and alive.
