Redpill Special

The Redpill Special is your starting weapon. In having it, you are offered a challenge: to maintain and upgrade your weaponry to prove to your faction their faith and investment in you. The Redpill Special gets the job done, assuming the job isn't too tough. It will serve you well, for a short time.




Royer Snub Nose

The Royer Snub Nose is about as loud, but hits a little harder than your first weapon. Its range is similar, and it handles notably better.




Royer Mk. II

As well-designed as its predecessor, the Royer Mk II is also equally as noisy, but packs a bigger punch.




FM-700

The FM-700 is a handgun with better than average accuracy. It has a maximum range and is concealable in combat comparable to lesser pistols.




FM-700 Magnum

The FM-700 Magnum handles well, with good balance and accuracy. It has decent range, and hits pretty hard.




Harlick Arms 363

The Harlick Arms 363 is noteworthy in its balance and accuracy, as well as its damage-to-target output.




Harlick Arms 464

Maintaining the excellent design of its predecessor, the Harlick Arms 464 pistol is both highly accurate and relatively quiet - at least as firearms go. It is a good, serviceable weapon for use against the opponents you are likely to face.




WestTek 49SA

FM-900

FM-900 Magnum

FM-1100 Magnum

WestTek 55ZA

Harlick Arms 717
