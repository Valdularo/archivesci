*Recover a dossier from a group of Exiles.*


Agent Gray: Mr. Kantai... I am very pleased that you have seen the logic of our ways. I assure you that you will not regret your decision. Now that you have agreed to work with the Machines, we have an assignment for you...

We require that you enter an Exile stronghold recently established in a local financial office. In the center of this stronghold is a two-room vault. Break into the vault and recover the dossier you find there. That document is quite important to us.

Operator: The Exile forces here are light, but you'll need to find card keys to access the vault.

In the last room you'll find a locked safe with the dossier. You'll need to find out the combination to the safe before you can open it.

Merovingian Soldier 1: I'll tear out your spine.

Operator: Check those bastards for loose change.

Operator: A key! That should get you past the first door.

Operator: More guards!

Merovingian Soldier 2: I hope you like pain, because it's all I have for you.

Operator: Search them for a key to the next door!

Operator: That cardkey should get you past the second door.

Operator: The safe is locked! Does it ever end?!

Merovingian Soldier 3: It ends here and it ends NOW!

Operator: Quick, check their bodies for a combination or something.

Operator: The combination is in that file foldier. You can unlock the safe now.

Operator: There's the dossier. Take it!

Operator: Good work, Liosazer. That's the dossier. Let's get this somewhere safe for Agent Gray.

Agent Gray: As your operator stated, you will be delivering the dossier to one of our other new recruits. His name is Dollfus. Meet up with him. Do not delay, Mr. Kantai.

Operator: Dollfus is another one of the former Zion recruits. Once you've delivered the dossier to him, all should be well.

Dollfus: I'm a little surprised to see another redpill so soon. It's a bit refreshing to know that there are others out there that think working for the Machines isn't such a bad idea. Anyway, on to business... do you have the Dossier?

Operator: We've got company, Liosazer! Unless I miss my guess, Dollfus' old crew is out for his head. Stop them from killing Dollfus!

Dollfus: Thanks for--Hey! Did you hear that? Oh, crap! They found me... The Zion operatives from my old hovercraft are upset with me for leaving Zion! Will you help me stop them?

Zion Virologist 1: Kill the traitors of Zion!

Zion Virologist 2: Dollfus is here! Kill him!

Operator: All Zion attackers are dead.

Dollfus: I'm in your debt, Liosazer. I hope we can work together again soon.

Agent Gray: You did extremely well, human. I can only hope that you continue to prove yourself as a reliable asset.
