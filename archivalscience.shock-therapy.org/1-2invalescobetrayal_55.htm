Communications intercept. Transmission detected Wed 05th of Sep 2007 at 04:10 AM by Mega City Times reporter, Kirrana. Communique follows:



After yesterdays events we sent one of our reporters Kirrana undercover to see what she could dig up on Invalesco. This is her account of what happened. :-

Through the grim twilight world of Manssen Park I followed the the dark faction of Digital Reality. Dark deeds were abound tonight and I felt a sense of foreboding as they lead me on a dance to the Church of Disciples. Hidden among the heights of the balcony I thought back to start of the day. A source had tipped me off that Digital Reality had been seeking the 4 items Invalesco had asked for. It was not hard to find them they did little of cover their tracks, and when they moved on mass I simply followed keeping to shadows.

They had come to meet Invalesco of that I was sure, what was to follow after that I doubted even they knew the answer. So I sat as they did waiting hour after hour ticked by but still nothing. It seemed nobody heard him turn up he appeared as if by magic on the stage.

"I am here" his booming voice rang out. "Clear the stage and sit down. Do you have the items I asked for?" a simply nod from the crowd indicated the answer was yes. "Good, Good, Who will be first to accept my power". A figure stepped forward answering the question "Me, my lord, Kiping". "So who is to be the sacrifice then" Invalesco asked. From the crowd a small figure was pushed to the front. He look nervous but summing all of his strength he reply " Me Megabyt".

Kiping and Megabyt took to the stage, handing the items to Invalesco. He place the items at his feet and turned to towards the two men on stage and said "Lets begin". Invalesco began to chant softly at first but gradually increase with power and pace. Words tumbled out of this throat, but not for humans ears. The disjoined sounds made no sense but the very pace of the words set a chill down my spine. Louder and Louder the chant became the pace quickened I felt dizzy now and wished it all to stop. To my surprised it did just stop for a heart beat. Looking over the balcony the two figures on stage looked at each other as if wondering what happens next.

Pink lighting shot down from the church roof hitting the standing figures they shock like puppets on strings failing about helpless as the lighting stuck again and again. I looked with horror at the agony on their faces etched with pain, they collapsed in a heap to the floor. They had paid a high price for the prospect of reward, paid with their own life.

Invalesco had not finished like the conductor of an orchestra his hands moved in motion to some unheard music. The bodies started to raise of the floor until they where fully upright. Kiping and Megabyt both open eyes looking down at their own bodies for any changes.

"What, What, this is not right. The spell it has failed how can this be ?" Invalesco shouted breaking the near trance of the crowd.

"That is very easy to answer brother. Your plan has failed now taste true defeat." It was Invalesco Bother Feronus now stood on the stage he lashed out striking Invalesco sending spinning to the ground. "Foolish brother I am not without power still" With that Invalesco picked himself up his hands raising upwards as if collecting a power source. Lighting once again shot from the roof this time hitting Feronus square on, leaving nothing but a burnt corpse.

Feronus might be dead but he had not come alone Tehort and Azrafel screamed in rage and drove at Invalesco. Out numbered he turned tail and ran towards the Hypersphere Monument. The crowd followed some trying to help him reach safety others baying for his blood. On reaching the Monument a battle broke out Blood Drinker vs Blood Drinker, as Invalesco battled Azrafel. His early performance must of drained his power he looked a beaten man and he knew it.

Taking a step back he jumped into the midnight air land directly on the Monument. From where I was stood I could just make out the figure standing on the monument. I could have been mistaken but to my eyes it looked like he opened a door. But what I am sure of is the figure Invalesco seemed to vanish into thin air.

Azrafel screamed in her rage "Damn he's gone, escaped, you redpills if you see him again contact us we will avenge Feronus death" With that she followed Tehort at speed down the street.
