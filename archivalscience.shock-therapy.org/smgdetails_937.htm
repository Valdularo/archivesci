Zatkin Machine Pistol

The Zatkin Machine Pistol is a little less wieldy than a single-shot pistol, but has greater range and can deliver more rounds to the target per pull of the trigger. Consequently, the weapon is louder and may attract more attention than a semi-automatic pistol




Zatkin Improved Machine Pistol

The Zatkin IMP enjoys greater range than a semi-automatic pistol, and fires multiple rounds per pull of the trigger, causing potentially greater damage. Because of its size and action, however, it is both less accurate and noisier than a single-shot handgun.




FM-9K

The FM-9 submachinegun has both maximum applicable range and damage capability greater than that of a pistol, but less than that of a rifle. It is significantly louder than a pistol, less noticeable than a rifle, and only moderately well balanced.




FM-9SK

Louder than a pistol, the fully automatic FM-9SK submachinegun also sports greater range. Also, a hail of bullets is a lot more damaging than one at a time.




D-A Arms Stitcher

The D-A Arms Stitcher isn't hampered by the short range of a pistol. However, neither is it blessed by a pistol's relative subtlety.




D-A Arms Zipper

The D-A Arms Zipper trades subtlety for range and damage as compared to a pistol.




Nishiki Fury

Compared to a pistol, the Nishiki Fury submachinegun has greater range, does greater damage, and will get greater attention from those in the area.




FM-11

More destructive and with better range than a pistol, this firearm will serve you well against your opponents.




FM-11SK

The best submachinegun you are likely to find, the FM-11SK is very hard-hitting.




FM-13SK

The FM-13SK is an improved version of the FM-11SK. It was first put in production over a year after the original to improve on the weapon's damage output.




Grishev R95

Just when many felt that Grishev would not be able to top their own PDW-43, the R95 proved to the critics that the manufacturer was not content to rest on its laurels.




Dual FM-9SKs

So long as you have the ability, wielding a pair of FM-9SK submachineguns is pretty straightforward, as the weapons are well-balanced and accurare. Although you're sacrificing any attempt at subtlety, this pair is very effective at removing opposition.




Dual Stitchers

In the case of wielding dual Stitchers, the sum is more than one-plus-one. While the weapons still sacrifice subtlety for range, not much can stand for long against this pair.




Dual Zippers

Two SMGs are less subtle than one - not that one is very subtle on its own. You will be noticed using this configuration. However, you'll also be putting your opponents down a lot faster than if you were armed with just one.




Dual Nishiki Furys

Wielding two well-balanced and accurate submachineguns gives you increased firepower and combat area presence.




Dual Nishiki Tempests

The Nishiki Tempest does more damage, at greater range, and with less subtlety than any equivalent pistol. With the appropriate skill, you can use two of them at once.




Dual FM-11s

With the appropriate skill, you can simultaneously use a pair of very deadly FM-11 submachineguns.




Dual FM-11SKs

With the appropriate skill, you can arm yourself with a matched pair of very hard-hitting submachineguns.




Dual FM-13SKs
