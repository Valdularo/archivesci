[Org Name] Archive - Mission [Number]

This device will launch an archived mission, copied from the [Org Name] historical database. May work only in certain city districts. Please note that events portrayed in this mission are not current events; all individuals and items have been recreated from database information, for educational purposes only. Upon successful completion of the mission, you will receive either a ticket for the next mission in the series, or a token for successful completion of the series. Make sure you have a few inventory slots open before you finish the mission.
