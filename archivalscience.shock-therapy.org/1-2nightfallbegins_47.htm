Communications intercept. Transmission detected Wed 05th of Sep 2007 at 04:06 AM by Mega City Times reporter, Kirrana. Communique follows:



Someone in the matrix has unleashed a new terror in Mega city tonight. This new terror took the shape of a pack of wolves and their handlers. Only a few hours ago a number of Exiles in the form of Lupines and Nosferatu that's werewolves and vampires to the uneducated amongst you attacked. Coming through the Mara Central hardline they burst out in front of a very bemused crowd of people. Their strange form and odd clothes picked them out as new comers right away.


One brave member from the crowd stepped forward addressing the female vampire know as Beherit.


"Welcome to Mega City Miss, what is it you seek can we help ?" he asked. The vampire looked at him and uttered the words "Blood... Blood.....Blood" Then with the smile pointed a boney finger towards the man and uttered one last word "Yours.....". The vampire was the first to attack she moved with lighting speed to the mans neck within seconds he fell to the floor an empty husk.


The spell was lifted from the crowd they reached for their guns as the lupine closed. Firing wildly the front rank of the crowd went down never scoring a hit the onrushing forms. The twisted bodies from the front row at least bought those from behind the time to prepare. It did little to help, the speed of the vampires and strength of the lupine could simply not be matched. Some people tried to bargain with the foes but to little avail they were not in a mood for much talking. One of the lupine did utter a few words in a deep guttural tone as much to his mates as to the crowd.


"We can not trust these. Drive them from the pack. Our Territory" The lupine then stopped and almost seemed as if they where smelling the air one uttered "It is not his pack our Alpha is not here" they turned and ran towards Tabor park. By the time we caught up with the lupine they had been joined by more vampires and the scene of death at Mara has already been repeated around Tabor park.


More redpills flooded the area trying to bring down the foes, but their was just as much fighting between the factions as the common foe it was a hopeless task. Tehort one of the lupine reached out grabbing a redpill by the throat smashing him to the ground. He turned to his fellow lupine and said "It is good to kill again. Humans understand so little." The killing carried on the better part of an hour before the disappeared into the night as soon as they had come no one left alive was willing to give chase.
