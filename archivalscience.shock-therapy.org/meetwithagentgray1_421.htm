Communications intercept. Transmission detected Wed 12th of Sep 2007 at 06:32 AM by Othinn. Communique follows:



*Meet with Agent Gray in the Tabor Park building in the Slums.*


Agent Gray: I wish to meet with you in person, Mr. Kantai. I will be waiting for you at the Machine building in Tabor Park. I expect you to be... punctual.

Operator: Agent Gray is somewhere in this area.

Agent 1: Keep moving, human.

Agent 2: All readings at this location are normal.

Agent 3: All indications are positive. You may proceed to your meeting with Agent Gray.

Agent 2 or Agent 3: Agent Gray is awaiting your presence, Mr. Kantai.

Agent 2 or Agent 3: Caution is advised, Mr. Kantai.

Agent 4: Agent Gray's location is secure.

Agent 4: Very few humans have earned the right to such a meeting, Mr. Kantai.

Agent 5: It is unusual for Agent Gray to meet with any of our operatives... personally, Mr. Kantai.

Agent 6: There is nothing to see here, human.

Agent Gray: There is one aspect of humanity that still confuses me -- Loyalty. As a program, I am part of a whole. I follow the commands of my code. I do not have a choice. That is not my nature. But you humans, you do have a choice. You have free will. And yet some of you inspire others to follow you, even to the exclusion of self-preservation.

If you wish to form a crew of your own, I will offer you my support. Studying it would be a valuable opportunity.

Agent Gray: That is all, Mr. Kantai.

Agent 2 or Agent 3: Your presence is no longer required, Mr. Kantai.
