Communications intercept. Transmission detected Tue 31st of Mar 2009 at 05:39 PM by MxO Resource. Communique follows:




*Today, MxO Resource brings you an interview with liaison officer Keterina of the Vector instance. We greatly thank Keterina for her time and we hope you enjoy the following interview!
Interviewee: Keterina
Position: EPN Liaison Officer
Date: 11/16/06
Location: Mjolnir Monument - Magog*

Resource: Why did you decide to join E Pluribus Neo?

Keterina: Once I was freed, I started hearing so many stories about Neo. How he kept going against all odds. The more I thought about it, the more I realized what a rarity that is. But E Pluribus Neo seemed to be a group of people who adhered to that mindset. To keep going when anyone else would fail. I wanted to be a part of that.

Resource: EPN's goals are often misunderstood by others. Can you clarify these goals for us?

Keterina: The way I see it, we work to uphold the memory of Neo and the freedom he gave us. I suppose that may be easy to misinterpret, but it's really pretty simple. Too many people want to take advantage of what they're given.

Resource: Recent surveillance scans indicate EPN may be in control of Neo's body. Any comment on this?

Keterina: Hrm, I haven't been given the green light to comment on this. I'll just say that it's not difficult to start rumors. Grains of salt should be in ample supply. :)

Resource: In the past there have been tensions between Zion and EPN. How do you see these tensions now? Are they improving, declining, etc.?

Keterina: Tensions ebb and flow between all organizations. It's hard to say how we'll feel from one day to the next. But I think we have a lot to offer Zion that would benefit them. They may be a bit hesitant to work with us, but I trust that our actions will speak for themselves.

Resource: Does EPN believe in the methods Morpheus used during his crusade to retrieve Neo's body?

Keterina: I'm guessing you mean the code bombing? Yeah, that's a doozy. I want to say that as an organization, we don't condone it. Code bombs are dangerous to bluepill and redpill alike. But, there will be certain people who fly the EPN flag who think code bombs are a necessary measure. Personally, I think we can make much more progress through non-destructive means.

Resource: Michael and Shimada...what are they really like? You spend more time around them than we do. How do their personalities reflect who they really are?

Keterina: I am in complete awe of Shimada. She's very goal oriented. Once she sets her mind to something, she means to see it through. She's also very compassionate. She cares about everyone in our organization, whether she says it out loud or not. As for Michael, he's a born leader. It's hard to deny that he really believes in what he's doing, and I have to admire that. Although, it makes him a little less personable. It takes a bit more to just sit down and have a chat with him.

*We at Resource would like to sincerely thank Keterina and EPN for providing Resource with this interview. We hope you found it informative.
