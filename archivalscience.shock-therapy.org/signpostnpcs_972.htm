Remember, when you enter Close Combat, choose your Tactics wisely. An experienced warrior analyzes their opponent's weaknesses in order to learn exactly what is required to defeat them.

The Speed or Burst tactic is best against opponents with low Toughness. Power or Aimed Shot will do the most damage against opponents with high Toughness.

Grab or Retaliate does a small amount of damage, but it has a chance of disrupting your opponent's concentration, causing them to lose any self-applied boosts or power-ups. Finally, Block or Evade does no damage, but helps you regain Inner Strength. Block also has a chance of removing any harmful conditions that may be affecting you.

Remember what I have told you, and you may just survive your first battle. Seek out Master [Name] when you have mastered Tactics.
