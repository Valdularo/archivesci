RSI stat reconfiguration hack

RSI reconfiguration hacks allow you to reconfigure an aspect of your RSI. This hack allows you to reset your stats and regain all spend stat points. To use the hack right-click to bring up a menu, then select use item. This item is consumed after a single use.




EMCP

This is an EMCP (Electro-Magnetic Code Pulse) device. When activated, it will disable all vulerable programs within 10 meters.




Experience Code Amplifier

This device may be used once at any time for an experience boost of 10% of the requirement for your next level.




Insecticide Code

A can of insecticide with a yellow label.




RSI Pill

This pill executes custom code that will permanently alter one of the components of your RSI's appearance, provided that you are of the matching gender.




Accelerator Bit

This consumable code bit salvaged from an Accelerator program will speed up all friendly targets in the area when used.




Decelerator Bit

This consumable code bit salvaged from a Decelerator program will slow down hostile targets in the area when used.




Runtime Bit

This consumable code bit salvaged from a Runtime program will spawn Accelerated Machine programs on hostile operatives in the area when used.




Terminator Bit

This consumable code bit will cause massive damage to nearby programs.




Patcher's Pill

A special pill obtained from the Fumigator, found near the Fly Virus infestation in Bathary Row. When swallowed, the code contained in the pill boosts health regeneration, and protects the user from Fly Virus infection for a short time.



Quicksilver Pill

This pill enhances your speed and reactions, enabling you to block and evade attacks more easily.




Overdrive Hypo

This hypo ramps up your adrenaline production and heightens aggression. This has the effect of increasing your damage output, but with the side effect of enraging you until the effects of the hypo wear off. This buff cannot be removed with shift+right click.




Code Optimizer Inhaler

This inhaler optimizes your ability codes, allowing you to use them for less inner strength.




Code Optimizer Hypo

This hypo boosts your maximum inner strength.




SSR Gum

The SSR group formulated this product to boost the efficiency of their security forces, and to keep them on the job when the situation at the research station began to deteriorate. The program siphons power directly from the Machines, temporarily increasing combat ability and awareness. Some of the energy feeds back to the user's jacked-in body, producing a pleasurable sensation of satiation that has been known to become addictive after long exposure. The user becomes more susceptible to the illusion of the simulated world, increasing their vulnerability to some hacks, and the stimulating effect of the program decreases the user's ability to concentrate.




Kunoichi Initiate Toy Box

Works in: Sati's Playground
Enemy waves: 10
Enemies per wave: 1
Time between waves: 10 seconds
Time limit: 1 hour
Disables: Concealment, Disguise, Escape and Evasion, Invisiblity, Master Shadow, Sneak, Vanish
Suggested team level: 30

This single-use toy box, from a friend of Sati's near Debir Court, can only be activated in the Sati's Playground archive construct. When used there, creatures will spring out and attack you one at a time. If you can defeat them all within the time limit, a reward will appear. The creatures may also drop other special items, but only if you or your mission team's level is less than or equal to that of the creatures you kill.

Note that if any other enemy who spawned to attack you, such as a Wasteland Corruptor, a level 100 Agent, or a creature from another box, is still active in the Matrix, no new creatures will appear when you activate this box, and you will not be able to earn the box's reward.
