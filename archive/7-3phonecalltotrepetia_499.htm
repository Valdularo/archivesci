Communications intercept. Transmission detected Thu 13th of Sep 2007 at 07:11 AM by Cloudwolf. Communique follows:



# Takes place just after receiving the field update from Zion Command informing all operatives of The Kid's role in saving the Oracle from assassination.

CloudWolf: I was glad to hear of the Oracle's safety. I suppose we owe one to EPN... as much as I hate to admit it.
Trepetia: Mm, it's been a rather good PR stunt for them.
Trepetia: As you rightfully said though the main thing is the Oracle's safety.
CloudWolf: Aye, it's looking to work out in their favour alright. I admit what he did was honourable but that doesn't change the reality of their situation with us. No matter if they want it to or not.
Trepetia: Wise words. I think some have lost sight of that.
CloudWolf: Trust me, I more than most understand the risks of what they're doing. They need to get a grip and head back to Zion before they get themselves killed. I would've thought Keterina would've made them see that...
Trepetia: Yeah, unfortunately some of them don't quite see the big picture.
Trepetia: Which, is perhaps why it is a good thing they are not with Zion.
CloudWolf: Perhaps...
CloudWolf: Though there are those who have simply lost their way. We should never close our arms to those who can understand their mistakes.
Trepetia: Yeah. Some EPN operatives are a credit to their organisation.
Trepetia: They would be an asset, if they were to return.
CloudWolf: Then we can only hope that with time and guidance they realise that the way forward for humanity will not be found by disregarding the truce.
Trepetia: Indeed, well I must depart for now, I'll be back in a few hours.
CloudWolf: I understand. It's been a pleasure, as always.
Trepetia: As always. Take care.
