Communications intercept. Transmission detected Wed 05th of Sep 2007 at 04:30 AM by Unknown. Communique follows:



*Tonight a party was held by Salve Veritate on the Proxy server. Members of all three alignments were in attendance. It was good to see, even if just for one night, Zionites, Machines and Exiles partying together as one. We had a wonderful time and a lot of fun (except for the occasional idiot who'd come in and attempt to start Pvp).*

*Then, completely unexpectedly, Niobe arrived at the party. We were all shocked. She was very casual during her visit and even stepped up to the bar to order a drink, though all she ordered was a water. She then proceeded to engage in conversations with us about Morpheus and the fight for Zion. Here are some excerpts from the conversations during the party:*

(missing exact phrasing) Niobe: Has anyone seen Morpheus?
Mixed responses mostly saying "No"

FootFknMaster: Niobe... pleasure to meet you finally.
Niobe: The pleasure is mine. I really have been wanting to swing this way for some time.
Niobe: You all gave me the reason to do so.

(missing exact phrasing) Niobe: How do you feel about Morpheus' actions?
*Mixed responses about how we are confused because we feel a connection to Morpheus but a necessity to protect Zion and the Matrix*
Niobe: Good that you are all speaking sense. He's a good man, a great man, but he needs to work with all of us, not having us work for him.

(missing exact phrasing) C0sair says something about Morpheus thinking he's God.
Niobe: He's not that bad, C0sair. I know he's a lot of things, but he doesn't have a God complex.

Tech3099: Niobe when was the last time you talked to Morpheus?
Niobe: A while back, Tech. He's not exactly on speaking terms with most of Zion right now.

Nanashiko: Niobe do you feel that we as supporters of Zion should follow Morpheus blindly or should we fight for the truce? Your advice would be greatly appreciated!
Niobe: Nanashiko, we are Zionites, not Morpheus-ites. We have a city to defend. Children, families to defend. The truce is vital to us all.
Niobe: Morpheus is a great man, but he risks us all.
Nanashiko: Thank you Niobe! That is exactly what I needed to hear!!!
Niobe: Glad I could clarify.

Murasame: Excuse me Niobe, are there any suggestions you can give us on the search for Morpheus?
Niobe: I wish I had some Morpheus hunting tips, but he eludes even his oldest friends.

Niobe: Ok guys, I hate to say it, but this was a quick visit. Give the hosts my utmost respect.
Niobe: What is the host's name again?

Kire: Niobe it was nice meeting you and I hope to work with you someday soon.
Niobe: Kire, I hope to work directly with you sometime
