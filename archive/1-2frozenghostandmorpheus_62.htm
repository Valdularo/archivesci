Communications intercept. Transmission detected Wed 05th of Sep 2007 at 04:29 AM by FrozenGhost. Communique follows:



FrozenGhost: Greetings Morpheus… I am FrozenGhost… leader of The Frozen… we have been giben order to bring you in for questioning regarding the recent posters and pamphlets being placed around the city… I can not believe that you are behind this… how should I proceed?

Morpheus: How do you think you should proceed?

FrozenGhost: I need more information which thus far has been sparse… I have noticed though that the imposter Agents have returned.
FrozenGhost: Is there anything you can tell me?

Morpheus: They were never gone.

FrozenGhost: Have they been biding there time? My crew and I have noticed increased activity around hardlines and throughout the mega city.

Morpheus: I am not the Oracle. I know little much like you. Bits and pieces.

FrozenGhost: I have very little and Lock is sharing even less… I feel… lost and so does my crew… we do not know who to trust and what orders to follow.

Morpheus: You already know what you must do.

FrozenGhost: We will continue to search for answers… I will follow the orders of Zion… but know that we also heed your direction.

Morpheus: I will remember you. Reclaim the Holy Place.

FrozenGhost: Until we speak again Morpheus… be safe… the Matrix is becoming increasingly dangerous for you.

Morpheus: And to you. I believe in you.
