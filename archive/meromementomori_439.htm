Communications intercept. Transmission detected Wed 12th of Sep 2007 at 06:47 AM by Screenshots made by SuperJenius. Communique follows:



Note: I'm assuming that the Operator's dialogue is the same in each org's version of this mission. It was the same in SuperJenius' screenshot as it was in mine, and I did the Zion version.


Flood: It seems that you humans have finally managed to kill off that pesky Assassin, but the mess he made lingers around the City. We might be able to put his corrupted data fragments to good use. Go get one of them.

Operator: The corrupt code reading comes from somewhere in that building.

Operator: Oh, gross! That's definitely where the code reading is coming from. Upload those flies to me at a hardline; I think I can decompile them. I'll upload the result to you if I get anything useful, but make sure that you have an inventory slot free before you upload the flies.

Flood: A fly in amber... A rather poetic touch for the Assassin, isn't it? I shed a single solitary tear. He was, at least, a little more entertaining than most of the freeware that the Merovingian sees fit to truck into the Matrix.
