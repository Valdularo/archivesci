Communications intercept. Transmission detected Tue 23rd of Sep 2008 at 10:32 AM by ArtificialMind. Communique follows:



I had been searching Mara for some time. I'd heard he might be somewhere around there, but I couldn't find him. That's when I realized I didn't need to see him to talk ... so I took a shot in the dark and sent him a message. To my shock he responded!

Quote:
AM: I know you're out there. I know you'll here me. But I don't expect a response. But I'm lost. Does Neo's body matter that much? Don't we all have it in us toi be that strong? That thread of the pattern has ended.
Morpheus: Without his body, how do we know if he is truly gone? Neo may yet live as a prisoner. I will not rest until he has been returned to us.
AM: At what price though? What Would you give to have him returned?
Morpheus: The One is everything. I will shake the very foundations of the Matrix until he is returned
AM: Are you strong enough? Can you accept the pain if you fail?
Morpheus: Everything happens for a purpose. I will not fail in my purpose.
AM: Won't there be another One, another Neo? Won't the cycle continue?
Morpheus: How can a cycle begin again, if the previous cycle never ended?
AM: What needs to be done?
Morpheus: I'm not ready to reveal my plans. But the time will come soon.
AM: We will be waiting.


Morpheus was as eloquent as I had ever heard him, but my language was sub-par - I was just in shock to be talking to someone so ... important!
