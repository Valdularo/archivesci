Links and Contact Details

  • Official Matrix Website

The original Matrix website. Features news, galleries, desktops, comics, and much, much more. What is the Matrix? The answer to that is different for everyone.


  • The Animatrix

The Wachowskis team up with several Asian animators to make nine animated short stories set in the Matrix universe.


  • Enter the Matrix

In the war to save Zion, what part will you play? A game that features Niobe and Ghost during the events of the Matrix sequels.


  • The Matrix Online

I'm not sure what this is. I hear it's an online game, but you have to pay to play. That sucks. ;P


  • The Matrix: Path of Neo

Play as Neo and move through the events of the trilogy, and even a few new places.


  • Metaurchins

A website that details the fan made ARG (Alternate Reality Game) based on The Matrix that ran back in late 2003. This was a lot of fun in my opinion.


  • ARGNet

An ARG website that has archived versions of the Metacortex ARG.


  • Netninja Archives

A site that archives many sites and files related to the Metacortex ARG.


  • MxO Resource

An MxO fansite that features a ton of helpful information, plus Cloudwolf's Storyline Guide and a Sentinel archive.


  • MxO Archive

Another fansite with lots of info, plus an archive of Data Node One and Stack's Journal, which features live event and mission dialogue.


  • The Matrix Online @ rumbaar.net

Yet another informative MxO fansite. Features a guide that emulates the ability discipline screens from the game, among other guides.


  • MxOStory.com

A site that features Data Node One's storyline articles, plus in-character commentaries and pictures from the webmaster: Super Jenius.


  • Paul Chadwick's Blog

The online journal of Paul Chadwick, the main writer of The Matrix Online. He is also the writer of Concrete and three Matrix comics, among other projects.
