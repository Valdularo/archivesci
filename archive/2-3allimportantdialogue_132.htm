Romav asked about getting the character dialog from the Death of the Destroyer event, so here goes.

This main chunk is from a doc written by Paul Chadwick.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~



1. Announcement on Data Node One

Death of a Destroyer

DATA NODE ONE ANNOUNCEMENT:

Time is ripe. The Assassin must be eliminated. It will take massive effort – he’s been infesting the Matrix like the insect swarm he is. His every aspect must be exterminated in proper order. Each Organization sees the wisdom in this. Or they will. Each will play its part.

Join in avenging the death of Morpheus – and the salvation of the Matrix.

Know this: things can get worse before they get better.

Day 2: Seraph Introduced Oracle

SERAPH: Greetings to you all. A grave situation has arisen. She wishes to address you. Please keep your distance, and accord Her respect. And above all, listen.

Day 2: Oracle Script

I don’t know if there were flies in the Garden of Eden. But we have them in the Matrix. It’s a problem.

These aren’t just pests. They spread a sort of disease. This program, The Assassin, is taking his function of disposal to extremes. The Matrix can be stressed beyond its ability to self-repair. This could be the time that happens.

I can’t see beyond certain choices. The future depends on what you choose.

Earlier, you chose well. You managed to destroy the Corrupted, the great man-shaped swarms. That was the first step.

Now smaller Corrupted, the RSIs of helpless Bluepills consumed by flies, stalk the Matrix. They must be eliminated. Only then, when these false aspects of The Assassin are gone– think of them as his projections into fourth-dimensional space – can his true aspect be destroyed.

That’s the one that wears the mask. It killed Morpheus. My friend.

That time will come, if now you can destroy the Corrupted.

Day 2:

ATTACK ON OOKAMI

System Message: The Assassin has attacked one of the Merovingian’s own. Ookami is in critical condition – why she is still living is a mystery. The Merovingian is at the end of his patience; he instructs his operatives to seek and destroy the Assassin – before the rival organizations do.

Day 3: Niobe

A promising avenue has opened. The Assassin fancies himself a historic figure. He’s written a memoir. Parts of it fall out of Corrupted as code bits. They’re readable, but fragmentary – and they seem to suggest where he haunts, mostly places associated with disposal. Trash. Sewage. Decay.

We think we have a way to kill him, but first we must know where to find him. We’ve got to collect those code bits and piece them together. Cooperate. Share information. That’s key.

Day 3: Agent Pace

Rosapillore, tutto ha senso ora. These code bits, shed by The Corrupted, they have clues to where The Assassin returns, the places he feels most vivo. He writes them himself, making his own grattacapo! We must collect them, assemble them, glean their meaning.

Soon a method of decompiling The Assassin will be at hand. But first, find him; assembling the code bits is the way.

Day 3: Flood

Attend me, my swashbucklers. It’s a sad day when an old friend flies off the handle and raises a stink. Regrettably, the Assassin broke all of Ookami’s fingernails and then some. Poor little bit…flea-bitten girl.

This just won’t do. Nobody messes with the Merovingian’s associates. You bon vivants must show the cave-bats and the mechadamia nuts how it’s done. Scoop up the code bits these Corrupted drop when you deliver the coup de grace. Assemble them. Read the night thoughts of porcelain-puss. Seems they tip off where he returns to clean his wings.

Soon we’ll have some delightful fly swatters for you.

Day 4:

System Message: Insecticide spray guns are now available from all organizations. Contact your controller to obtain them. This is the only way to kill the true aspect of The Assassin. You want one.

Day 5:

System Message: The Assassin has split into five aspects. Each has human form and porcelain mask. They may be weaker for splitting. But they have scattered to different locations, believed to be those hinted at in the code-bit messages. Good hunting.

Day 5: Niobe

Congratulations. You did it. The killing blow. The Assassin is no more. Look at the sky. You are entitled to the mask; take it. We’ve checked it; no flies. Keep it as a trophy. It’s yours, forever.

I thank you for helping to avenge Morpheus.

Day 5: Pace

Buono! The Matrix mends itself, thanks to you! It was your blow that finally felled the Assassin! Take his mask as a memento. It is free of his noxious code. You need not fear infection. It is yours forever. Look at the sky, clearing!

Day 5: Merovingian

I am grateful that you helped clean up this little problem. It seems you landed the killing blow, hmm? Not too bad. I have something for you. The porcelain mask; take it. A little keepsake. It has been sterilized, fear not. It is yours forever.

Day 5: Niobe

Niobe: We did it. The Assassin is no more. It’s confirmed. Morpheus, we did it for you. (NIOBE WEEPS, RECOVERS)

There’s a lot of credit to go around. If you landed a blow, let it be known. You deserve to be written in the history. And make no mistake, this was an historic event. Machines, Zion, even that French piece of trash code who created this problem – and his day will come, I promise – all worked together. For the first time. Because the Matrix, whatever else it is, is a commons. And the logic of the commons is the same whether it’s a stretch of land or an ocean or an atmosphere. If it’s threatened, the only way to save it is to unite in shared purpose.

So go, celebrate. This is a victory that will be remembered forever.

Day 5: Agent Pace

Thanks to you, the system heals, the Matrix endures. All Rosapillore, be they Machine, Zion, or Merovingian associates, seized the day and saved our precious existence. If you landed a blow, make it known; you deserve your place in the archives.

Let this be remembered; humans and machines can work together, unite, survive. It is the only avenue. We have tried war. It leads only to destruction. All consciousness must coexist.

Now, be allegro, my friends! You have saved your world!

Day 5: Merovingian

We have done well here. We have saved the Matrix from a noisome destruction, and done it with panache. All credit to you, my cavaliers. This insectile inconvenience was not a trivial challenge; indeed, dealing death was his specialty. That you brought him down fills me with – dare I say it? -- a fatherly pride.

There is grumbling from the churls in the other organizations that The Assassin was brought to the Matrix by me. Let them carp. It was the Machines who made him, after all…and he fed every pod-born like a mother nurses her infant; how very ungrateful!

That aside, this is a glorious day. Look at that sky. Not a fly, not a gnat amid its open, gusty expanse. Smell it; it is the clean scent of victory, and we share it today.





NPC RUMOR Here’s the latest: The Assassin’s Achilles heel is his egotism. He’s writing a memoir as he runs. A little legacy project. People find pieces. Get that memoir, or its fragments (then assemble them), and you’ll find what you need to find and kill him. If we can’t bring him down, well, stay indoors. The sky’s not going to be pretty.
