Communications intercept. Transmission detected Sat 22nd of Nov 2008 at 09:25 AM by Othinn. Communique follows:





Chaste, the collector for the Guillotines gang


Initial Message

I was hired to kill the Guillotines. While I'm not one to shy away from a little wet work, full scale slaughter has never been my style. If you're interested in subcontracting, bring me the Red Silk Ribbons that they carry. For this, you will be adequately compensated.

If you don't have enough gang tokens

A bullet from a mile away, a poison tab in a drink, or my bare hands in the dead of night... these are my stock and trade. This "mass carnage" that my employers want brought against the Guillotines is a different... artistry. Bring me more of these Red Silk Ribbons since you seem to have this style of artistry within you.

If you have all of the requirements

It would seem to be I'm a victim of your success. The contract I currently hold to eliminate the Guillotines has been extended. I suppose acting as middleman can have its perks. Should you gather more Red Silk Ribbons, feel free to bring them to me.

After the trade

Hmm... curious. It would seem my, and by proxy your, benefactor is none other than the Pendhurst-Amaranth Corporation. Normally I would never reveal an employer, but seeing how they're paying us to eliminate a gang on their payroll, I decided that cautionary word was warranted.




Father Jonas, the collector for the Legion gang


Initial Message

Demons... demons walk among the bluepills. You might think them just Exiles, but the Legion spreads corruption and vice wherever they go. I don't want to know how you do it, but bring me their Pentagram Amulets - the very symbol of their evil ways.

If you don't have enough gang tokens

Please, you must collect their symbolic amulets. The Legion will continue spreading its filth and corruption unless we fight to stop them. Know that you will be tangibly as well as spiritually rewarded when you bring me a full collection of the Pentagram Amulets.

If you have all of the requirements

Bless you my child, you have brought Pentagram Amulets from the demon Legion. I know it must have been... rough, but you know that you've done a service to all souls still trapped in the Matrix. The war is far from over, but this renews my faith that it is a war that just might be won.

After the trade

Some have questioned me: is destroying these Exiles' lives really a work of benevolence; or are the greater evils of fear, hate and prejudice just wrapping themselves in a cloak of righteousness and using me as their pawn? To them I say - my faith shows me the truth! I was right about the Matrix, and I will continue to prove I am right this time as well!

The Crushers took the only friend I've ever known and my gang won't go after them because it isn't profitable. I don't care about money, I want revenge!
