Communications intercept. Transmission detected Tue 04th of Sep 2007 at 09:21 PM by DJ Dakari. Communique follows:



Seraph: Hello, Dakari. May we speak?
Dakari: What would you like to speak about?

Seraph: I would like to speak to you and the members of your faction about the current state of the Truce.
Dakari: Where can we meet you to discuss the current state of the Truce?

Seraph: May we meet in 15 minutes? And the place can be anywhere of your choosing.
Dakari: 15 minutes would be a great time. Does the location need to be private, or can it be public?

Seraph: Whereever you choos. Contact me again in 15 minutes.
Dakari: I will scout a location, and we will talk again in 15 minutes.

Seraph: It is almost time.
Dakari: indeed it is. Would you like the location now?

Seraph: Yes, please.
Dakari: The rooftop of the building that is directly behind the Mara Northeast hardline.

Seraph: I see. I am on my way.
Dakari: I will speak with you soon.

Seraph: Thank you for gathering on this short notice.

Dakari: The pleasure is mine.

Seraph: Those who would be a friend to Humanity attend to my words. I know that many of you believe you know where my heart is, but loyalties are complex things, like eddies in a river. Always flowing and moving forward. When the way comes to an end, then like water, you change. Having changed, you pass through. In the past, being a friend to humanity was to be a friend to Zion alone. But that time is gone.

Ingus: Greetings, Seraph.

Seraph: Greeting. I am in the middle of a speech, but please, listen in. Some of you have made your peace with your enemies, even joined with them. In this you are no less human, and no less deserving of my respect. I am not the Oracle, I cannot see the future. But even a blind man can smell the smoke of a fire. Even though the Truce is intact, conflict is never far. The flames kindle once more, even now. Even when the world is at peace, held together by the tenuous threads of the Truce, a learned man still keeps his sword by his side. If that time should come again, I want you to know that I will not be an enemy to those who wish my friendship, no matter which master they serve. Your deeds alone will guide my esteem for you. But if you wish to harm the Oracle, or any others under my protection, you shall find me a formidable enemy. Do not believe that your master's power can protect you from me. I dislike death, however, there are some things I dislike more than death. Victory is reserved for those who are willing to pay its price. My back bears the scars which prove I will sacrifice that which is most precious to me in order to follow my heart. The Truce has shown the best in humanity. But it has also shown the darkest side of man. There is not one among you that has not been victimized by this. And yet, even the victim can be the aggressor. But remember, even now in the face of increasing hostility, the Truce remains intact. While each of you must choose your own way, it does not matter how slowly you go, so long as you do not stop.

Traxada: Betraying us now Seraph?
Warwraith: It seems to me that it is the Zionists that no longer want peace.
Ingus: We all have pats we must take, Traxada. It was Zion that lashed out on us, even now as we speak.
Seraph: Please do not mistake me, Traxada. I have not betrayed anyone. Peace is not easy, and my message must be delivered to many others.

Traxada: You spoke of not bringing harm to the Oracle, but yet these Merovingian dogs are the ones that threaten her very life.
Ingus: He has his reasons.
Seraph: Ingus, some people are taking advantage of the situation of the Truce. Be strong and do not give up your cause.

Traxada: You dissapoint me Seraph.

Dakari: The Oracle is one of our kind. She may have helped Zion to defeat the Machines, but that does not mean that she supports Zion.

Seraph: I have told them that if they intend to harm the Oracle, I will be their worst enemy.
Traxada: Then you have already made an enemy. Can you not see?

Ingus: Seeking the destruction of the Matrix, means the end of all exiles.
Dakari: The Oracle included.

Seraph: We will speak again in the future. Farewell.

Vdera: Farewell, Seraph.
Ingus: Farewell.
Dakari: Farewell, Seraph.
